
# My Favorite Movie: Titanic

## About the Movie
**Titanic** is a romantic movie released in 1997. It tells the story of Jack and Rose.

## Movie Information
- **Title:** Titanic
- **Director:** James Cameron
- **Genre:** Romance and Drama
- **Year Released:** 1997

## Main Characters
1. Jack Dawson
2. Rose DeWitt Bukater
3. Cal Hockley

## Why I Like This Movie
I like *Titanic* because it has a beautiful love story. It teaches us about love, sacrifice, and courage.

## Lessons I Learned

- Life is precious.
- We should help other people.
- Money cannot buy true happiness.

**10/10** - A beautiful and unforgettable movie!
